Logic & How It Works

This calculator estimates fabrication man-hours using realistic engineering formulas.

Shell Fabrication Logic

  • Plate Cutting: Length × cutting rate
  • Edge Preparation: Plate perimeter × edge prep rate
  • Rolling: Diameter × thickness factor × rolling rate
  • Fit-up: Shell circumference × fit-up rate
  • Welding: Total weld length × welding process rate ÷ joint efficiency

Nozzles

Nozzle hours = (Fit-up + Welding) × quantity × reinforcement/flange factors.

Supports

Support hours = quantity × base rate × support type factor.

Painting

Painting hours = surface area × total coats × painting rate × blasting/insulation factors.

Productivity Adjustment

Final hours are adjusted by shop productivity, welder skill, and automation level.

Total Hours

Total = Fabrication Hours + Additional Fabrication Hours.
