End of Service Calculator

Calculate your End of Service Benefits based on salary and years of service. This tool follows common GCC HR standards.

How End of Service is Calculated

End of Service (EOS) benefits are typically calculated based on your last drawn salary and total years of service. Many GCC countries follow a formula similar to:

Formula:
EOS = Salary × 0.5 × Years (first 5 years)
EOS = Salary × 1 × Years (after 5 years)

Example

If your salary is 5,000 SAR and you worked for 3 years:

EOS = 5000 × 0.5 × 3 = 7,500 SAR
