Round Bar

Weight = π × d² / 4 × Length × Density

Pipe

Weight = π × (OD² − ID²) / 4 × Length × Density

Plate / Sheet

Weight = Length × Width × Thickness × Density

Square Bar

Weight = Side² × Length × Density

Rectangular Bar / Tube

Weight = Width × Height × Length × Density (minus hollow core for tubes)

Angle (Approximation)

Weight ≈ (Leg A + Leg B − Thickness) × Thickness × Length × Density

Channel / Beam (Approximation)

Weight ≈ Cross-sectional area × Length × Density, where area is estimated from flange and web dimensions.

All dimensions are converted to meters before calculation.
