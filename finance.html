Loan Calculator

How It Works

The loan calculator helps you estimate your monthly payment, total interest, and total repayment amount. It uses the standard amortization formula, which spreads payments evenly across the loan term.

Loan Calculation Logic

Every loan payment includes two parts:

  • Principal — the amount you borrowed
  • Interest — the cost of borrowing money

As you make payments, the interest portion decreases and the principal portion increases.

Formula Used

Monthly Payment (EMI) is calculated using: 

    EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

    Where:
    P = Loan Amount
    r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
    n = Total Number of Payments (Years × 12)

Example Calculation

Example:

  • Loan Amount: 10,000
  • Annual Interest Rate: 5%
  • Loan Term: 2 years

Monthly interest rate = 5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.004166 Number of payments = 2 × 12 = 24

Monthly Payment = 438.71 Total Payment = 10,529.04 Total Interest = 529.04

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed monthly amount you pay until the loan is fully repaid.

Does this calculator include compound interest?

Yes. The formula uses monthly compounding, which is standard for most bank loans.

Can I print the results?

Yes. Use the print button in your browser or add a dedicated print button if needed.

Is this calculator accurate for all loan types?

It works for personal loans, auto loans, and most bank loans. It does not apply to mortgages with variable interest rates.

How It Works

The gold calculator multiplies the weight of gold by the live market price per gram. When you select 24K, 22K, or 21K, the calculator adjusts the price automatically.

Gold Calculation Logic

  • 24K: Pure gold (100%)
  • 22K: 91.6% purity
  • 21K: 87.5% purity

Price per gram is calculated as: 

    Price_per_gram = Live_24K_price × (Purity / 24)

Example

Example:

  • Weight: 10 grams
  • Purity: 22K
  • Live 24K price: 280 SAR

22K price = 280 × (22/24) = 256.67 SAR Total value = 256.67 × 10 = 2566.70 SAR

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the price update automatically?

Yes, the calculator fetches live gold prices from Saudi Arabia.

Is 24K the purest gold?

Yes, 24K is 99.9% pure gold.

Why is 22K cheaper than 24K?

Because 22K contains alloy metals for strength.

Can I print the gold value?

Yes, use your browser print option.
