How the EOSB Calculation Works

Based on Saudi Labor Law, the End of Service Benefit is calculated using your salary, years of service, and reason for leaving.

  • First 5 Years: EOSB = 0.5 × Monthly Salary × Number of Years (up to 5 years).
  • After 5 Years: EOSB = 1 × Monthly Salary × Additional Years.
  • Total EOSB: Sum of first 5 years benefit and additional years benefit.
  • Termination: Employee receives 100% of Total EOSB.
  • Resignation:
    • Less than 2 years: 0%
    • 2 to 5 years: One Third (33.33%)
    • More than 5 years up to 10 years: Two Thirds (66.67%)
    • 10 years or more: 100%
